# v3.0 Phase 2 outcome — every v2.x augmentor variant fails k-fold validation

**Date:** 2026-06-03 (Phase 2 run completed)
**Status:** Phase 2 complete — 8 experiments re-evaluated under 6-fold k-fold CV; **0 of 8 produce a robust win**
**See also:** [`2026-05_v3.0_phase1_kfold_design.md`](2026-05_v3.0_phase1_kfold_design.md), [`2026-06_v3.0_phase1_smoke_outcome.md`](2026-06_v3.0_phase1_smoke_outcome.md), [`2026-05_v2_closing_summary.md`](2026-05_v2_closing_summary.md)
**Branch:** `claude/v3.0-kfold-cv` (8 commits ahead of main, pushed)

## TL;DR

The 7 PR C single-split experiments + the committed PR B baseline were re-run under the v3.0 6-fold k-fold harness. **Not a single experiment shows a robust "Model B beats Model A" result.** 6 of 8 have Model B *worse* than Model A on the per-fold mean; the best result (`pr_c_e4_density_plus_curation`) is essentially flat at mean −0.036 c/L with stdev 0.396 — well inside the noise band.

This is the methodology-validation outcome v3.0 was designed for: **PR B's v2.x committed "Model B beats A by 0.24 / 0.32 c/L" headline was a 2-fold-specific artifact, and PR C's experiments were sub-tuning against fold noise.** The entire v2.x augmentor surface, as currently configured + modelled, adds no robust value over Model A.

## What we learned

1. **The v2.x augmentor surface is methodology-validated as non-robust.** Every variant explored across PR A/B/C — temporal DSS, temporal GCP, new ERP density column, curation broadening, all 4 combinations — fails the k-fold significance threshold. The closest to a positive result (E4) is mean −0.036 c/L, an order of magnitude below the per-fold standard deviation.

2. **Single-fold ranking led us astray for ~3 weeks.** v2.x's PR C ablation interpreted E4 (best test_crisis) as the strongest signal; under k-fold, E4 has roughly the same mean as E5 (worst single-split combination). The ranking from single-split doesn't match the k-fold ranking.

3. **The fold-to-fold variance is structurally large.** Even within a single experiment configuration, per-fold Δ MAE can swing from −0.6 to +1.7 c/L (e.g. PR C E1: min −0.645, max +1.743 — a 2.4 c/L span). Some folds genuinely favour Model B, others genuinely don't. The single-split methodology happened to pick favourable folds; an adversarial split would have shown the opposite headline.

4. **Curation broadening (PR-A's 5 unmodeled candidates) is harmful on average.** E4b (curation only, no density) has the highest stdev-normalised Δ MAE → the 5 cols added in PR-A introduce signal that doesn't generalise. They were ranked by gain importance from a single fit; that ranking was fold-specific.

5. **The new ERP density column is also harmful alone** (E4a: mean +0.506). It only "works" in combination with the curation candidates (E4: mean −0.036) — and even then, the combined "win" is noise-band. Some structural interaction makes the joint less bad than either alone, but neither component is robust.

6. **DSS-temporal vs cross-sectional makes no robust difference.** E1's mean (+0.218) matches PR B's (+0.215). The expensive temporal pass over DSS we added in v2.x is contributing nothing detectable.

7. **GCP-temporal makes no robust difference** either (E2 mean +0.155 vs baseline +0.215 — both noise). The 2016 vs 2021 Census per-row resolution doesn't move the needle.

## Why v3.0 Phase 2 took 7+ hours wall-clock (not 3)

The first run (yesterday) stalled mid-fold-5 of e3 after the laptop slept. Restart-from-cache logic (see `tools/research/v3_phase2_kfold_runner.py::_run_experiment`) saved the 3 completed experiments and resumed with e3. Even so, the second run hit another laptop sleep during `pr_c_e4b_curation_only` (210 min wall-clock vs ~27 min normal). The resume-safe logic now records the wall-clock-from-sleep as the actual time so future analyses don't over-attribute slowness to LightGBM.

**Lesson for v3.0 Phase 3 (Docker handoff):** the home AMD server doesn't sleep. Even modest cost in per-experiment compute is worth it for elimination of the sleep-restart loop.

## What this means for the project — three readings

Phase 2 results admit three quite different interpretations, each with different next-step implications. **These are research / discussion items, not decisions yet.** See [`docs/research/2026-06_v3.0_phase2_postmortem_discussion.md`](2026-06_v3.0_phase2_postmortem_discussion.md) for the deep dive I'm following up with — including a literature check on time-series CV variance interpretation, Nadeau-Bengio correction, and what tree-based forecasting projects typically find.

### Reading A — the augmentor surface is genuinely flat

LightGBM on already-feature-rich tabular forecasting may have nothing left to extract from demographic context. The lag/upstream/calendar/weather/station blocks already capture most explainable variance; SA2 demographics may be redundant with what `stn_*` features encode implicitly through per-station price history.

**If true:** ship Model A as the production model, retire the SA2 augmentor dependency, archive the v2.x feature work as a methodology study rather than a feature contribution.

### Reading B — v3.0 methodology is too strict

The design doc's "|Mean| > Stdev → robust" heuristic is intentionally conservative; naive paired t-test on k-fold is dependent-samples broken (Dietterich); per-fold variance in time-series isn't really "noise" in the IID sense — it's actual environmental shift between time periods. The Nadeau-Bengio corrected t-test might give different answers.

**If true:** revise §2.5 of the design doc; recompute corrected significance; some of the v2.x findings may rise above noise.

### Reading C — wrong model / wrong feature engineering

GBM with fixed hyperparameters might be the wrong capacity to extract augmentor value. The Centrelink-day × SEIFA interaction hypothesis (spec §13 #3) needs an explicit interaction feature; trees don't find it. Or the marginal-value problem needs a different algorithm class entirely.

**If true:** invest in hyperparameter tuning, interaction features, or different model families (NN, GAM) before declaring the SA2 surface dead.
