## TL;DR

**Phase 1 harness works end-to-end** — 6-fold k-fold CV against the committed PR B features completed in 21 min wall-clock + 30s evaluate. Per-fold + aggregate report at [`results/v3_phase1_smoke_kfold.md`](../../results/v3_phase1_smoke_kfold.md).

**The bombshell:** under k-fold CV, **Model B (with SA2 augmentor block) LOSES on average vs Model A.** Mean Δ MAE = **+0.215 c/L**, stdev = **0.394 c/L**. The v2.x committed PR B headline ("B beats A by 0.24 c/L on test_normal, 0.32 c/L on test_crisis") **does not survive k-fold validation** — it was a 2-fold-specific artefact, exactly the failure mode the v3.0 methodology was designed to detect.

This is a finding worth pausing on before continuing. v3.0 was motivated by "we need to test whether v2.x wins are robust"; the first run already says some weren't. Phase 2 still re-evaluates the other PR A/B/C experiments to see which (if any) survive.

## Why this differs from v2.x

| Difference | v2.x single-split | v3.0 k-fold | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test window scope | 2024-25 + 2026 only (2 years total) | 2020-2026 (6 years rotating) | k-fold covers harder periods (2022 Russia-Ukraine, 2026 spike) where demographics don't help |
| Target leak | `y_t1[D]` on last train row D where D+1 ∈ val/test | `gap_days=1` drops the leak | Small effect on absolute MAE; not the cause of the +0.45 swing |
| Train length per fit | ~6 years (2017-2022) | Folds 1-6: ~3, ~4, ~5, ~6, ~7, ~8 years | Earlier folds have less train; possible underfitting confound |
| Fold periods | "Normal" 2024-25 specifically | All including high-volatility windows | Folds 3 + 6 specifically capture price-shock periods that destroy the augmentor's value |
| Aggregate signal | Single Δ MAE | Mean ± stdev | Variance signal makes "robust vs noise" judgable |

## What the segmentation tables show

`results/v3_phase1_smoke_kfold.md` includes segmentation by metro/regional, brand, fuel type, SEIFA quintile — computed across the union of all folds' test predictions. Same pattern: small Δ MAE values, mixed signs across segments, no segment robustly favours B.

## Caveats / things to verify before declaring v2.x dead

1. **Earlier-fold underfitting hypothesis.** Fold 1 trains on ~3 years (2017-2020) vs PR B's 6 years. LightGBM with `n_estimators=2000` + early stopping might be making different model-complexity decisions per fold. Worth checking the `kfold_audit.json` for best-iteration variance.

2. **Fold 6 dominates the mean.** Δ MAE +1.042 on fold 6 (2026 spike) is far above any other. Drop fold 6 → mean drops to ~+0.050, much closer to neutral. The 2026 period is qualitatively different from the rest of the panel; arguably k-fold including it gives a more honest picture, but it's worth noting that ~85% of the "B loses on average" signal is one fold.

3. **`gap_days=1` is a behavioural change.** v2.x train_end's last row had `y_t1` overlap with val/test_start. In v3.0 that row is dropped. Per-station, that's a 1-row-per-quarter trim. Unlikely to materially shift the mean by 0.45 c/L but worth quantifying the rows-dropped count in a follow-up.

4. **Single run.** Smoke confirms harness works; doesn't claim definitive results. Phase 2 re-evals will repeat under known-config changes (PR A pin bump, PR B temporal, PR C E4 density+curation, etc.).

5. **Crisis-fold drop is honest.** v2.x reported test_crisis Δ MAE −0.321 (B won) which sat alongside test_normal in the headline. v3.0's fold 6 (the closest analog) shows Δ MAE +1.042 (B lost by a lot). The reversal is REAL given the methodology change — v2.x's `test_crisis` was 2026-01 → 2026-04 (4 months); v3.0's fold 6 test is 2025-05 → 2026-04 (12 months including the lead-up to the spike). The longer window changes the picture.
